# Phase 11 — Sales Invoices & Receipts

Implemented on top of Beta 3 / Phase 10.

- Completed Sale opens a professional invoice/receipt document.
- Invoice history supports View, Print and Download PDF.
- Invoice includes business logo/details, customer, invoice number, date/time, line items, quantities, unit prices, discount, VAT/tax, total, amount paid, balance/change and payment method.
- Business profile now supports phone, email, address, Tax PIN/VAT number and logo URL.
- Added dependency-free client-side PDF generation for invoice downloads.
- Print opens a clean print window so the browser can print to paper or a system PDF printer.
